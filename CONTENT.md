# Free content for the mapper

Hand-picked animations and videos for Diwali and Halloween that you can load into a zone
(press **P**, then pick a file or paste a link). Collected October 2026.

**What projects well:** bright shapes on a **pure black** background. Black is "no light", so only the
glowing part lands on the wall. Anything with a white or coloured background shows up as a lit rectangle.
Backgrounds below were judged from titles and descriptions, so check the preview before you rely on one.

## How to load each kind

| Kind | Where from | How |
|---|---|---|
| Lottie animation | LottieFiles | Open the link, choose **Download → Lottie JSON** (a free account may be needed), then pick the `.json` file in the panel |
| Video file | Pixabay, Mixkit | Download the MP4, pick it in the panel. HD (1080p) is plenty and plays smoother than 4K |
| YouTube | YouTube | Paste the link in the panel. Only works when the mapper is opened with a start script (`start-mac.command`, `start-windows.bat` or `start.sh`) |

## Licences in one line each

- **LottieFiles free animations:** Lottie Simple License. Personal and commercial use, no credit required. A few creators set their own terms, so glance at each page.
- **Pixabay:** Pixabay Content License. Free, no credit needed. Don't resell the clips as they are, and avoid clips showing brands for commercial use.
- **Mixkit:** Mixkit License. Free download, no watermark. Read the licence linked on each clip.
- **YouTube:** embedding is allowed by YouTube, but public or paid shows using someone else's video may need their permission.
